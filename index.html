NotifyHub Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 9, 2026

The short version: We don't collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Information Collection

What We Access

Notification Content: The App reads notifications from other apps on your device using Android's NotificationListenerService. This includes sender names, message previews, app names, and timestamps.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect, upload, or transmit any notification content to any server
  • We do not collect personal information, email addresses, phone numbers, or account details
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs
  • We do not collect device identifiers or location data

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Notification history — local SQLite database
  • Statistics (daily counts, response times) — local storage
  • Settings and preferences — Android DataStore
  • VIP contact markings — local storage
  • No cloud storage, no remote servers, no data sync

Data Retention

  • Notification entries are kept until dismissed or the app is uninstalled
  • Statistical data is automatically cleaned up after 30 days
  • Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all stored data

Permissions

PermissionPurpose
Notification AccessRead notifications from other apps to display them in NotifyHub
Foreground ServiceKeep the notification listener running in the background
Post NotificationsSend reminder notifications for overdue messages
BillingProcess in-app purchases through Google Play

In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time "Pro" upgrade through Google Play. Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not process or store any payment information.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Google Play Billing for in-app purchases, subject to Google's Privacy Policy. No other third-party services are used.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

NotifyHub 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 3 月 9 日

簡單來說：我們不收集、儲存或傳輸您的任何個人資料。所有資料都留在您的裝置上。

資訊存取

通知內容：本應用程式使用 Android 的 NotificationListenerService 讀取您裝置上其他應用程式的通知，包括發送者名稱、訊息預覽、應用程式名稱和時間戳記。

我們不收集的資訊

  • 我們不會收集、上傳或傳輸任何通知內容到任何伺服器
  • 我們不會收集個人資訊、電子郵件、電話號碼或帳號資料
  • 我們不會使用分析、追蹤或廣告 SDK
  • 我們不會收集裝置識別碼或位置資料

資料儲存

所有資料僅儲存在您的裝置本地。沒有雲端儲存、沒有遠端伺服器、沒有資料同步。

資料保留

  • 通知記錄保留至用戶關閉或解除安裝應用程式
  • 統計資料在 30 天後自動清除
  • 解除安裝本應用程式將永久刪除所有儲存的資料

權限

權限用途
通知存取讀取其他應用程式的通知以顯示在 NotifyHub 中
前台服務保持通知監聽器在背景運行
發送通知發送提醒通知（30 分鐘未回覆訊息）
帳單透過 Google Play 處理應用程式內購買
